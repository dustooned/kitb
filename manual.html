Quick start (5 minutes)

  1. Open the class link: Kit Forge. Fill in Kit info (kit title, your name or team, class).
  2. In the gallery, tap ＋ New and pick a card, piece or board size. Tap any thumbnail to open it; ‹ Gallery takes you back.
  3. Decks: drag one thumbnail onto another to stack them into a deck (like folders on a phone). Drag between thumbnails to reorder, drag out of an open deck to take something out. On a touchscreen, press and hold first. Right-click (or press and hold without moving) for rename, duplicate, move and delete.
  4. Choose a size preset (poker, tarot, square, 8×8" board…) or type custom inches.
  5. Build the design from layers: image, text and shapes.
  6. Press Export ZIP to hand it in, or Send to Table to playtest it.
Your work autosaves in this browser on this computer only. Export a ZIP before you leave a school computer.

Working with layers

StudiosThe top of the tool rail switches studio, like Affinity: Layout (frames, text, placing art, shapes), Vector (pen and shapes), Pixel (brush, eraser, colour picker, cut out). Keys 1 2 3.
Tool groupsA tool with a small corner triangle holds more (e.g. Rectangle / Ellipse / Star). Press and hold, or right-click, to pick; the slot remembers your last pick.
DrawingShapes and frames: drag on the canvas to draw (Shift keeps it even), or just click for a default size. Text: click where it should go. Pen: click points, click the first point to close, Enter for an open line, Esc cancels.
PaintingBrush paints on the selected picture, or on a new Paint layer. Eraser removes pixels. Alt-click or the Colour tool picks a colour. [ ] change brush size.
MoveDrag the layer on the canvas. Pink guides show when it snaps to the centre or an edge (hold Alt to skip snapping).
ScaleDrag the corner handle.
RotateDrag the top handle.
Zoom / panCtrl + scroll or the zoom pill to zoom. Pan with the hand tool, Space + drag, or drag empty space while zoomed.
Order / hide / lockIn the Layers list, drag the dots to restack; the eye hides, the lock stops accidental nudges.
Duplicate / deleteButtons under Arrange, or Ctrl+D / Delete.
FramesThe Frame tool (or any shape → Turn into a picture frame) holds a picture clipped to its shape, like InDesign. Place picture or drop one onto a selected frame, then Fill / Fit, Zoom, and double-click to move the picture inside.
Right-clickRight-click a layer (on the canvas or in the list) for quick actions: convert a shape or picture to a frame and back, turn an SVG into a recolourable shape, duplicate, restack, lock, hide, delete.
SVG / vectorImage tool → pick an .svg. It stays sharp at any size. Right-click → Convert to shape to recolour it or use its outline as a frame.
Free resizeHold Shift while dragging a corner to change width and height separately.
Blend & effectsBlend modes (Multiply, Screen, Overlay…), plus Drop shadow, Glow and Sticker outline under Effects.
Remove backgroundSelect an image → Remove background. Best on plain backgrounds; Strength tunes it, Original undoes it.

Pieces come in circle, square, rounded square, hex and diamond. Uploaded art trims its transparent border automatically.

Importing art

FileWhat happens
PNG / JPG / WebPAlways works. Becomes one image layer.
Photoshop .psdLayers stay separate.
AffinityCan't be read directly. In Affinity: File → Export → PSD, then import that.
Illustrator .ai / PDFComes in as one flat image.
kit.json / Kit Forge ZIPReopens a saved kit so you can keep editing.

Use Import in the top bar, or drag files onto the page.

Saving & exporting

Export ZIPEvery card, piece and board as a PNG, a print-and-play sheet, kit.json (your editable save) and a table file for SAGA. This is your hand-in.
Print cardsA US Letter sheet with every card at its real size. Print at 100% (not "fit to page").
Send to TableDownloads a .kittable.json for SAGA.
To move your work to another computer: Export ZIP → take the ZIP along → Import it there.

Playtesting in SAGA

  1. Press Send to Table (or unzip your export and find the .kittable.json).
  2. Open SAGA and join your group's room.
  3. Drag the file onto the table, or use 📦 Load kit….

See the SAGA manual for table controls.

Troubleshooting

My work is goneYou're on a different computer or browser, or the browser data was cleared. Import your last ZIP.
The page looks outdated after an updateReload the page twice.
ZIP export or PSD import does nothingThey need internet the first time. Check your connection and try again.
Printed cards are the wrong sizeSet print scale to 100% / "Actual size".
